Connect your AI Mode activity

Use this connector in Opera. It sends captured Google Activity and opened AI Mode Details to your local Neural App. It can also capture visible Gemini and Flow text when the paired account identity is present. It reads no cookies or passwords. Keep this app and your signed-in browser running. Gmail automatic access is not included.

Your extension ID:

In Neural App → Google Activity, enter this ID and your account, then create a private pairing key.

Neural App and this browser must remain running. Permission does not make private data public or subscribe other devices. Remove the extension and revoke its pairing in the app to disconnect fully.
