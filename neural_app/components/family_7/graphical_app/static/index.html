THE GENERATION RULE

Drive = tanh(chiral weight × (field output + intention activation) ÷ 2). Positive drive rotates left rows clockwise and right rows counterclockwise; negative drive reverses both. Magnitude sets 1–2 positions per tick. Near zero holds the pattern.
