## 4 · Oneness and the vulnerable being

Your benchmark asks that every being be loved, with special attention to those least able to protect or represent themselves. The ethical bridge from interconnectedness to that benchmark is an additional commitment: another creature’s experience matters, so our actions should take its suffering seriously.

Interdependence alone is descriptive; care is a chosen response to it. A learner can explore oneness while retaining their own identity, judgment and freedom to disagree. A meaningful connection respects the other being rather than absorbing its choices into the operator’s wishes.

## 5 · The lion and the lamb: agreement and difference

Around 40 minutes the captions describe predation within a wider account of transformation. Your lion-and-lamb image asks for safety and affection where predation would otherwise threaten the vulnerable. These are related through shared life, but they are not the same ethical conclusion.

My application: universal care should include both the threatened creature and the predator’s needs.
