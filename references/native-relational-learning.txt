Rules are discovered from a finite one/two-premise hypothesis family. All
training-consistent candidates must pass the separate validation cases.
Competing predictions remain visible; disagreement asks for more observations.
A counterexample deactivates the model. New background facts recheck retained
labels. Receipts detect accidental changes to the checked model.

This is bounded symbolic induction, not neural weight training or independent
verification. Missing derivations do not establish that a relation is false in
the world. A prediction does not authorize any external action.
