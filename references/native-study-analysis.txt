For missing outcomes, design or disjoint subgroups:
analyse study: {"groups":[{"name":"A","events":30,"total":100,"missing":5},{"name":"B","events":20,"total":40,"missing":10}],"design":"observational","independent":true}

The denominator is the number with recorded outcomes; missing is additional
participants with unknown outcomes. Missing-data bounds explore all assignments
of those unknown outcomes, not sampling uncertainty. Events need not be good.
Strata must sum to the overall counts for both groups, including missing counts.

Per-group binomial Wilson intervals use the NIST formula:
https://itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section2/prc241.htm
They assume independent binomial observations and are withheld for declared
clustered/repeated observations. They are not an uncertainty interval or
significance test for the difference. No causal effect or individual forecast
is certified by the computed rates or a randomized design label.
