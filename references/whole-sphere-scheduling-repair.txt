WHOLE-SPHERE ACTIVITY AND FAIR SCHEDULING

The function worker now uses bounded fair slices of up to eight registered
roles per pass (three under resource pressure), rather than a fixed three-role
pass for all conditions. Every role retains its next turn after slow work.
Slow or failed role snapshots receive a measured temporary cooldown.

The shared training worker also uses fair slices instead of running its entire
service list before yielding. Existing source-fetch intervals, model policies,
disabled-state handling and resource-pressure limits remain authoritative.
Choice/Action/Consequence updates are owned by the existing sphere-function
worker, rather than waiting behind long capability suites in the training
worker. There is one writer for those studies and no extra model worker.

Original, selected and combined projections receive a lightweight refresh at
most once per second during conversation. Heavy autonomous processing still
yields to the user's conversation. This prevents downstream sphere views from
being fed only stale root frames while a chat is active.

Single-lane neural, rotated-plane and Choice/Action/Consequence views copy only
the requested lane. Function workers receive a bounded whole-mind snapshot and
only the workspace fields they consume, rather than copying unrelated history.
The full visual and source records remain in their original services.

Numeric channel observation rotates through captured source batches. Role
freshness, measured cost, retry delay and scheduler visits are available in
functional_work.scheduler_health. The visible sphere status shows freshness.
Choice/Action/Consequence views explicitly indicate idle passes when there is
no new source data; duplicate captures are not fabricated learning events.

Tests cover all registered role turns, slow and failing callbacks, source
coverage, conversation refresh, and avoiding unrelated frame copies. Freshness
is not intelligence: each role's evidence and experiment scope still applies.
